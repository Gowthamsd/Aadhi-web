Hassle-Free Turnkey Service

Our 5-Step Turnkey Installation Process

From factory manufacturing in Manjavayal to finished boundary installation on your land — we handle everything.

01

Site Measurement & Quote

We assess your land boundary perimeter in feet, discuss post height (8ft, 9ft, 10ft) and give you a transparent rate estimate.

02

Factory Casting & Curing

High-tensile steel rebar reinforcement mesh is placed into precision moulds and poured with vibration concrete, followed by water curing.

03

Transport to Your Land

We load and safely transport all RCC posts, plates, and materials directly to your site across the ECR and surrounding villages.

04

Pit Digging & Post Alignment

Holes are excavated every 6 feet. Posts are plumbed with laser/string line alignment and firmly cast with concrete footings.

05

Plate Sliding & Joint Sealing

Reinforced 6-ft RCC plates are slotted cleanly between the posts, leveled, and sealed for a solid, lifetime boundary wall.
